# 🛍️ Myntra Fashion Analytics Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](Myntra%20Analytics%20Dashboard.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

The Myntra Fashion Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze product performance, brand trends, customer preferences, ratings, and category-wise revenue insights.

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of fashion retail data, helping users understand product distribution, brand popularity, customer ratings, and sales patterns across different categories.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze product distribution across categories
- Evaluate brand performance and customer ratings
- Monitor monthly order trends
- Compare category-wise revenue contribution
- Identify top-performing brands and product segments

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- 🛒 Total Products
- 🏷️ Total Brands
- 📦 Total Orders
- 💰 Average Product Price
- ⭐ Average Ratings

### Interactive Filters

- Date Filter
- State Filter
- City Filter
- Category Filter
- Color Filter

---

## 📈 Visualizations

### Top Categories by Products
Displays the number of products available across major categories.

### Top Brands by Products
Shows the distribution of products among leading fashion brands.

### Average Rating by Brands
Compares customer ratings across top brands.

### Monthly Product Orders
Tracks product order trends throughout the year.

### Revenue by Category
Highlights category-wise revenue contribution.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Women and Men categories contain the highest number of products.
- H&M and Roadster are among the leading brands by product volume.
- Monthly orders show seasonal fluctuations throughout the year.
- Highly rated brands tend to attract stronger customer engagement.
- Revenue is concentrated within a few major categories.
