# 🍔 Swiggy Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Swiggy Restaurant Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze restaurant performance, customer ratings, delivery efficiency, pricing trends, and city-wise restaurant distribution.

This dashboard helps uncover valuable insights about food delivery operations and restaurant performance across multiple cities.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

* 🍽️ Total Restaurants: 8K
* 🏙️ Total Cities: 9
* 🛵 Average Delivery Time: 54 Min
* ⭐ Average Rating: 3.66

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### Top Rated Restaurants

Identify the highest-rated restaurants based on customer ratings.

### Restaurant Distribution by City

Analyze restaurant presence across different cities.

### City Wise Average Price

Compare average food prices between cities.

### Average Delivery Time by Food Type

Understand delivery efficiency across various food categories.

### Food Wise Average Delivery Time

Identify food categories with the highest and lowest delivery times.
