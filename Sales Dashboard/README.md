# 📊 Sales & Profit Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales & Profit Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, order trends, and channel-wise business performance. This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights through effective visualizations and KPI tracking.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance
* Analyze revenue contribution across sales channels
* Track monthly revenue trends
* Identify top-performing products
* Evaluate order distribution across channels
* Analyze revenue distribution by currency

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

* 💰 Total Sales: **155M**
* 📉 Total Profit: **58M**

## 🚀 Key Insights

* Wholesale channel generated the highest revenue and order volume.
* Revenue peaked during March and remained strong through September.
* Significant revenue contribution came from NZD and USD currencies.
* A small number of products generated a large portion of total revenue.
* Sales performance varied across channels, highlighting business growth opportunities.
